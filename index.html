Who We Are

Law, Creativity & Strategic Thinking Under One Vision.

Rossis Legal is a modern legal practice founded around the vision of David Nzeakor, a lawyer, writer and creative professional committed to combining legal knowledge, strategic thinking and an understanding of business and culture to create meaningful value for clients.

As a legal practitioner, David provides legal counsel and representation across areas including corporate and commercial law, intellectual property, entertainment and sports law, technology and digital law, dispute resolution and regulatory matters. Beyond legal practice, he is also a writer and contributor to conversations around ideas, creativity, business, law and society. Through Rossis Legal and its wider creative initiatives, he works at the intersection of law, business, intellectual property, entertainment and creative enterprise.

His approach is grounded in understanding the individual or business behind every legal matter. Whether advising a company, protecting intellectual property, supporting a creative professional, structuring a business relationship or navigating a complex legal issue, the objective remains the same: to provide clear, practical and strategic guidance built around the client's interests.
